Model Comparison Notes
======================

1. CSP + SVM
------------
Protocol: Leave-One-Subject-Out across 9 subjects.
Mean accuracy: 0.3889.
Accuracy standard deviation: 0.1329.

2. ShallowFBCSPNet
-----------------
Protocol: Leave-One-Subject-Out across 9 subjects.
Mean accuracy: 0.4670.
Accuracy standard deviation: 0.1598.
Mean Macro-F1: 0.4296.

3. Deep4Net
-----------
Protocol: Run-wise cross-validation inside 0train, then held-out 1test evaluation.
Subjects: [3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9].
Mean accuracy: 0.4623.
Accuracy standard deviation: 0.1444.
Mean Macro-F1: 0.4180.
Macro-F1 standard deviation: 0.1644.

4. EEGNet
---------
The existing EEGNet records contain training and development-stage results, but no final evaluation using a protocol that matches the other completed experiments. It is therefore marked as pending.

5. Interpretation
-----------------
CSP + SVM and ShallowFBCSPNet can be compared more directly because both use the same LOSO protocol.
Deep4Net answers a different question: within-subject generalization from 0train to 1test.
Its accuracy should not be interpreted as directly higher or lower than the LOSO results without a protocol-matched experiment.
